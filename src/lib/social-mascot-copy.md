# Maggie Social / Ad Copy Concepts

Channel-specific mascot copy for Meta, Nextdoor, and organic social posts. Keep the existing brand voice — refined, understated Southern hospitality — but add Maggie’s neighborly warmth.

## Concept 1: The neighbor recommendation

> **Headline:** Your neighbor Maggie knows a thing or two about a guest-ready home.
> **Body:** Hi, neighbor! I’m Maggie with Magnolia Maids. If you’re hosting at Lake Texoma this summer, let us handle the turnover so you can handle the s’mores. Flat-rate lake-house cleanings from $119.
> **CTA:** Book a summer turnover
> **Use for:** `/summer-on-the-lake` campaign, Nextdoor ads, organic Facebook posts

## Concept 2: The SOSU weekend helper

> **Headline:** Graduation weekend? Maggie’s got the before-and-after covered.
> **Body:** Family coming in for SOSU graduation? I’ll help you get the guest room ready before they arrive and reset the house after they leave. Pre-Event Prep + Post-Event Clean = 10% off when you book both.
> **CTA:** Reserve the Host Weekend bundle
> **Use for:** `/post-event-cleaning`, spring graduation push, email banner

## Concept 3: The referral invite

> **Headline:** Tell a neighbor. You both save $25.
> **Body:** Hi, neighbor! I’m Maggie. Refer a friend to Magnolia Maids and you’ll both get $25 off — no limit, no expiration while you’re on the schedule. It’s our favorite way to grow in Bryan County.
> **CTA:** Get my referral link
> **Use for:** `/referral-week`, social shares, customer thank-you emails

## Concept 4: The new-homeowner welcome

> **Headline:** New house? New start. Maggie can help.
> **Body:** Moving in or out of Durant, Calera, or around Lake Texoma? Let’s make it inspection-ready and deposit-safe. I’ll walk you through the flat-rate price in about 60 seconds.
> **CTA:** Get a move-out quote
> **Use for:** `/move-out-cleaning`, Nextdoor ads, organic posts

## Voice reminders

- Always identify as “Maggie, your Magnolia Maids neighbor.”
- Never invent prices, reviews, or years in business.
- Keep “neighbor” language natural, not forced.
- Pair Maggie copy with the magnolia bloom brand mark — never replace it.
